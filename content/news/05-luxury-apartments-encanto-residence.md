**Luxury apartments in Ulaanbaatar** are rare — especially in a **34-floor residential tower** with direct access to a premium mall, Grade-A offices, and a Grand Ballroom. **Encanto Trade Center - Residence** brings international residential standards to **Bayanzurkh District**, inside **Mongolia's tallest mixed-use development**.

## A 34-floor residential tower with skyline views

Encanto Trade Center - Residence rises above the retail podium with panoramic views across Ulaanbaatar. The tower is designed for residents who want privacy, prestige, and a connected urban lifestyle at one address.

## Reservations now open: floors 10–30

Reservations are open on **floors 10–30** with **Type A** and **Type B** layouts — two units per floor. Type A offers north, south, and east-facing orientations; Type B offers north and south-facing layouts.
