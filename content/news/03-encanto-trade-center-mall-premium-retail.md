**Shopping in Ulaanbaatar** is about to reach a new standard. **Encanto Trade Center Mall** — also known to many as **Central Mall** — is the retail heart of Mongolia's tallest mixed-use development, offering six floors of premium shopping, dining, and entertainment under a naturally lit atrium.

## A mall designed for international brands

Encanto Trade Center Mall is strategically located within **Encanto Trade Center**, the **tallest building in Mongolia**, in **Bayanzurkh District**. The mall connects office tenants, residents, and visitors through a glass-bridge podium that links the office and residential towers.

What sets the mall apart:

- **200+ stores** across six retail floors
- **8-level parking** for shoppers and tenants
- Premium **gastronomy** and food court offerings
- **International luxury brands** and flagship retail opportunities
- Direct access to [Encanto office](/office) and [Encanto Residence](/residence)
