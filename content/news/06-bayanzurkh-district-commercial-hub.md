**Bayanzurkh District** has long been one of Ulaanbaatar's most dynamic neighborhoods. Today, it is entering a new chapter as **Encanto Trade Center** — the **tallest building in Mongolia** — reshapes the district skyline and attracts national and international tenants.

## Why Bayanzurkh matters for business

Located in the **26th Khoroo** along **Ikh Mongol Uls Avenue**, Encanto Trade Center sits in a corridor with strong connectivity to the city center, government institutions, and major transport routes. The project is approximately **900 meters from Sukhbaatar Square**, placing it among the most strategically positioned commercial developments in the capital.
