**Encanto Trade Center** is more than the **tallest building in Mongolia** — it is a statement about how the country's capital can build at international scale.

## 135 meters, 35 floors, one vision

The tower stands **135 meters** tall with **35 floors**, making it the **tallest steel-frame building in Mongolia**. Its architecture combines a metal structure with a full **YUANDA glass facade** — the world-leading brand known for panoramic glazing, UV protection, acoustic insulation, and reduced thermal loss.

## Mixed-use by design

From the ground up, Encanto was planned as a **mixed-use destination**:

- **8 podium levels** with retail mall and underground parking
- **Office tower** with 24 floors of Grade-A commercial space
- **Residential tower** with 34 floors of luxury apartments
- **Encanto Grand Ballroom** on floors 7–8 of the podium

This vertical integration is rare in Ulaanbaatar and positions Encanto as a model for future **urban development in Mongolia**.

## Engineering for performance

### YUANDA glass facade

The facade delivers floor-to-ceiling views while maintaining comfort in Ulaanbaatar's climate — critical for both [office tenants](/office) and [residents](/residence).

### FUJITEC smart elevators

Seven **FUJITEC EZ Shuttle** elevators serve the office tower with intelligent routing for peak-hour efficiency.

### Smart parking at scale

**1,500 parking spaces** support office, mall, and event traffic — a differentiator in a market where parking often limits commercial growth.

## Shaping the Ulaanbaatar skyline

As construction advances, Encanto Trade Center is already defining the **Ulaanbaatar skyline** in **Bayanzurkh District**. For anyone researching **Mongolia's tallest building** or the future of **real estate in Ulaanbaatar**, Encanto is the reference point.
