Whether you are a growing startup or an established enterprise, Encanto provides premium office environments that elevate productivity, collaboration, and corporate identity. The [office tower](/office) spans **24 floors** with flexible floorplates, refined finishes, and panoramic views across the capital.

- **4.5 m** floor-to-ceiling height
- **7** FUJITEC EZ Shuttle smart elevators
- **1,500** parking spaces (including dedicated tenant allocation)
- **24/7** intelligent access control
- Enterprise-grade fiber connectivity

## Why lease office space at Encanto Trade Center?

### Prime Bayanzurkh location

The tower is located in **Bayanzurkh District**, one of Ulaanbaatar's most valuable commercial zones — close to government, finance, and major transport corridors.

### Connected to mall, dining, and wellness

Office tenants and their teams stay connected across the development without leaving the site. The [Encanto Trade Center Mall](/mall) offers retail, food court, and everyday services. Podium-level restaurants, meeting rooms, a conference center, and access to Encanto Sport Complex support daily business life.

### Entire-floor options for headquarters

Enterprise clients can configure **entire-floor headquarters** with customized interior planning. Selected units support flexible layouts to match corporate requirements.
