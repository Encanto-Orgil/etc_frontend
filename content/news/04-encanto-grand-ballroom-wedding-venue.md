For couples, brands, and institutions searching for a **wedding venue in Ulaanbaatar** or a **luxury event hall in Mongolia**, **Encanto Grand Ballroom** delivers an experience worthy of the capital's new skyline.

## A world-class venue crafted for extraordinary events

Encanto Grand Ballroom is a **world-class venue crafted for weddings, galas, conferences, and extraordinary celebrations**. Located on floors 7–8 of the Encanto Trade Center retail podium, the ballroom spans **1,600 m²** with **7.6 m ceiling height** and **Skyfold automated partition walls** — allowing one venue to host intimate dinners or grand receptions for up to **1,600 guests**.

## Why choose Encanto Grand Ballroom?

### Inside Mongolia's tallest mixed-use tower

The ballroom sits within **Encanto Trade Center**, the **tallest building in Mongolia**, in **Bayanzurkh District** — a landmark address that signals prestige to every guest.

### Flexible Skyfold halls

Skyfold partitions create multiple room configurations without sacrificing ceiling height or acoustic quality. Host a plenary session by day and a seated gala by evening in the same space.

### Full-service event support

Dedicated event coordination, premium catering partnerships, and AV infrastructure support corporate and social programs at international standards.
