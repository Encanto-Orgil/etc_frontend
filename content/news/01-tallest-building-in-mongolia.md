When people search for the **tallest building in Mongolia**, one name defines the answer: **Encanto Trade Center**.

Rising **135 meters** across **35 floors** in **Bayanzurkh District, Ulaanbaatar**, Encanto Trade Center is Mongolia's tallest steel-frame tower — and the country's most ambitious mixed-use destination. From premium office floors and a six-level retail mall to the Encanto Grand Ballroom and a 34-floor residential tower, the project brings luxury living, business, and lifestyle into one connected address.

## Why height matters for Mongolia's capital

Ulaanbaatar's skyline is evolving fast. Encanto Trade Center was designed not only to set a new height record, but to anchor **Bayanzurkh** as a district where international-grade architecture meets everyday urban life. The tower's **YUANDA glass facade** and metal structure reflect a commitment to world-class engineering in a market that has long needed Grade-A commercial and residential stock.

## What's inside the tallest building in Mongolia?

Encanto Trade Center is more than a single-use tower. It combines:

- **[Premium office spaces](/office)** — 24 office floors with 4.5 m ceilings, FUJITEC smart elevators, and 1,500 parking spaces
- **[Encanto Trade Center Mall](/mall)** — six floors of premium retail, gastronomy, and entertainment with 200+ stores
- **[Encanto Grand Ballroom](/ballroom)** — 1,600 m² of event space for weddings, galas, and conferences
- **[Encanto Trade Center - Residence](/residence)** — luxury apartments on floors 10–30 with skyline views

This integrated model is what makes Encanto the **tallest building in Mongolia** and one of Central Asia's most complete urban developments.

## Location: Bayanzurkh, 26th Khoroo

Encanto Trade Center sits on **Ikh Mongol Uls Avenue** in the **26th Khoroo of Bayanzurkh District** — approximately 900 meters from Sukhbaatar Square and positioned in one of Ulaanbaatar's most connected commercial corridors.
